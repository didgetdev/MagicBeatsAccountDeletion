Account Deletion - Magic Beats

Magic Beats users can delete their accounts and associated data directly within the app.

To delete your account:

  1. Sign in to Magic Beats.
  2. Open Account from dropdown in the top left corner.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

When an account is deleted, the user profile and associated content are permanently removed from Magic Beats.

If you no longer have access to your account or cannot sign in, you may request manual account deletion by contacting support at magicbeats@didgetsphere.com.

Data Deleted

When an account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Email address
  • Password credentials
  • User profile information
  • Uploaded beats and related files
  • Account preferences and settings
  • Device identifiers associated with the account

Data That May Be Retained

Certain records may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, or regulatory purposes.

  • Server logs and security records
  • Payment-related records processed through Stripe

Retention Period

Account data is deleted as soon as reasonably possible after a deletion request is processed. Records that must be retained for legal or regulatory reasons may be kept for the period required under applicable laws and then permanently deleted.
